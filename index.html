VAPOLLO VMISSION CONTROL · 01
MOON · SIGNAL RESCUE

One descent.
One signal to restore.

LUMA-1 is fictional. This lander challenge turns NASA’s measured Moon gravity into a playable decision: steer inside the signal ring, spend thrust carefully, and land with fuel to spare.

Apollo V is the team name. This is a simplified educational simulation, not an Apollo mission reconstruction.

MISSION LOGIC SIMULATION
01SteerLine up with the gold signal ring
02BrakeUse short engine pulses to slow down
03RescueLand softly; conserve your fuel
MOON SURFACE GRAVITY1.62 m/s²
MEAN EARTH DISTANCE384,400 km

NASA’s lunar-gravity value drives the fall model. Fuel, scale, and scoring are simplified for play—not flight engineering.
